How the model worksOne rule for every protocol +
Implied FDV = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier

01 The snapshot sets the multiple

For each category, use the median market cap / TVL of the snapshot's own tokenized protocols with a nonzero reported market cap and at least $5M TVL. A category needs at least three peers; otherwise it uses the global median across all eligible tokenized protocols.

02 Growth and time in market

Clamp 7d growth to −60%…+120%, then use 1 + growth / 100 × 0.5. Maturity is 0.6 + min(age in days, 1,095) / 1,095 × 0.4. Age is time since first listing; unknown age uses 365 days and unknown growth uses 0%. Multiply at full precision, round once, and floor the result at $1.

03 Launch probability

A heuristic, with no forecast horizon: start at 25%. Add 25 percentage points at age ≥730 days, or 15 at ≥365. Add 15 points for 7d growth ≥20%, or 5 for growth ≥0%; subtract 15 for growth ≤−20%. Clamp to 5%…90%. Unknown age counts as 365 days; unknown growth counts as 0%.

04 Expected airdrop assumption

Expected airdrop = round(implied FDV × 7.5% × launch probability). This is the typical-farmer proxy under an assumed 7.5% community allocation spread across the protocol's early users. The snapshot has no user count: the formula leaves the allocation undivided, so this is not an individual payout estimate.
